9. WHAT IS INSTALLED AND DOWNLOADED
Bubat AI installs into your user folder and needs no administrator rights. On first start it can download and install Ollama (an AI engine, about 1.6 GB, from ollama.com) and AI models (about 3 to 20 GB, from ollama.com) that fit your computer. Updates are downloaded from the project's official GitHub releases, verified with a checksum and tested before they are installed. Apart from the data described in section 8, Bubat AI does not send your account details anywhere; it reads news from public websites.
